# Loadline — User stories

**Product:** [PRODUCT.md](./PRODUCT.md)


### Pricing actuary

- As a pricing actuary, I want each model to declare the premium term it moves, so that I can see whether a proposed change belongs in the expected-claims basis, the risk margin, or the expense allowance.
- As a pricing actuary, I want the permitted variation range and frequency for a jurisdiction shown before I design a price, so that I do not build a structure the market will not allow us to file.
- As a pricing actuary, I want technical cost and commercial price recorded separately with the calibration attributed, so that our margin decisions remain visible rather than buried in the rate table.
- As a pricing actuary, I want realised experience for engaged cohorts against the assumption I priced, so that the engagement saving is confirmed or withdrawn on evidence.

### Underwriter and medical referral assessor

- As an underwriter, I want to see which factors drove a risk score and how they map to our rating basis, so that I can accept, refer, or override on an informed basis.
- As a medical referral assessor, I want cases involving restricted health data to be blocked from automated scoring, so that we never underwrite on evidence we are not permitted to use.
- As an underwriter, I want an automated declinature to carry an explanation I can give the applicant and a route to human review, so that a decline does not become a complaint we cannot answer.

### Analytics and data science lead

- As an analytics lead, I want candidate models queued for validation with their declared premium term, so that the speed of model production does not outrun our ability to permit models.
- As an analytics lead, I want a feature blocked at deployment when its permitted-use position is missing or negative, so that a proxy problem is caught before it reaches a customer.
- As an analytics lead, I want consent coverage per data source reported as a live number, so that a model's real-world applicability is known before it is priced into a product.

### In-force and engagement programme owner

- As an engagement owner, I want touchpoint frequency measured per member against the assumed frequency, so that the risk-loading case is supported by what actually happened.
- As an engagement owner, I want claims and persistency experience for my cohort side by side, so that I know whether the programme is creating or destroying value.
- As an engagement owner, I want a defined fallback when a member withdraws consent, so that they keep their cover and we keep a defensible price.

### Claims assessor and fraud investigator

- As a claims assessor, I want automated claims decisions confined to the coverage and evidence patterns that have been cleared, so that speed does not create wrongful declinatures.
- As a fraud investigator, I want coverage analytics and anomaly patterns surfaced with the evidence trail, so that a referral is defensible rather than a score.

### Reinsurance manager and compliance officer

- As a reinsurance manager, I want treaty experience reported by cession and cohort against the pricing basis, so that I can renegotiate or requote from evidence.
- As a compliance officer, I want to withhold a permitted-use position for a factor in a specific market, so that a model runs where it is lawful and is blocked where it is not.
- As a compliance officer, I want engagement programmes reviewed against the medical-advice and inducement boundaries per jurisdiction, so that a prevention service does not create a conduct exposure.
